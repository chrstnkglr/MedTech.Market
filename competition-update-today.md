## U.S. will not exit North American trade deal in 2026

**Author:** Nick Paul Taylor  
**Source:** [MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/trump-usmca-2026-exit-notice-medtech/824375/)  
**Publication date:** July 2, 2026

The U.S. has reportedly not issued the required notice to leave the U.S.-Mexico-Canada Agreement in 2026, meaning the trade framework remains in place ahead of the next formal review cycle. For MedTech manufacturers, this maintains short-term predictability for North American production networks and cross-border supply chains. The article highlights that industry stakeholders are watching issues such as tariffs, rules of origin, export controls, critical minerals, data flows and AI governance, all of which could affect device manufacturers’ operating models and cost structures.

**Strategic relevance:**  
**Trend indicator / Risk:** Continued USMCA stability supports near-term supply-chain planning, but the 2027 review remains a strategic watchpoint. Imaging OEMs with North American sourcing, assembly, service logistics or software/data dependencies should monitor whether future trade negotiations introduce cost, compliance or localization pressures.
