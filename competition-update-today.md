## Production problems hamper Lantheus bid for FDA approval of radiopharma dx kit

**Author:** Joseph Keenan  
**Source:** [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/production-problems-hamper-lantheus-bid-fda-approval-radiopharma-dx-kit)  
**Publication date:** June 29, 2026, 8:31am

The FDA issued a complete response letter for Lantheus’ NDA for LNTH-2501, a Gallium-68 edotreotide PET diagnostic kit intended to localize somatostatin receptor-positive neuroendocrine tumors in adult and pediatric patients. The agency cited unresolved manufacturing-related conditions at a third-party facility, while Lantheus stated that the FDA did not raise issues with the submitted safety, efficacy or clinical-performance data. The product is designed as a two-vial kit for radiopharmacies, enabling preparation of Ga-68 edotreotide using gallium from an on-site generator.

**Strategic relevance:**  
**Trend indicator / Risk.** The development underlines that molecular imaging competition is increasingly shaped not only by scanner performance and software workflows, but also by radiopharmaceutical manufacturing reliability, regulatory execution and supply-chain resilience. For PET/CT and theranostics strategies, control over tracer availability and quality systems is becoming a competitive enabler rather than a back-end operational issue.
