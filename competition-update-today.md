## Evidence Review of FDA, MAUDE, MHRA and EU Records Finds Independent Ultrasound Servicing Safe, Cheaper and Essential

**Author:** Rongtao Medical  
**Source:** [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/evidence-review-of-fda-maude-mhra-and-eu-records-finds-independent-ultrasound-servicing-safe-cheaper-and-essential-302810619.html)  
**Publication date:** June 25, 2026, 09:00 ET

Rongtao Medical published an industry report arguing that independent ultrasound servicing is safe, lower-cost, and operationally necessary for maintaining aging imaging fleets. The company cites FDA, MAUDE, MHRA, OECD, EU procurement, and other public datasets, including claims that only a very small share of adverse-event reports were plausibly servicing-related, that servicing-root-cause ultrasound recalls were limited, and that EU public buyers awarded most named maintenance contracts to non-OEM servicers. It also highlights cost differentials for ultrasound probe repair versus refurbished or new replacement units.

The release is commercially motivated, but it reflects a competitive theme with direct relevance to imaging OEMs: hospitals and public buyers are under pressure to reduce lifecycle costs and may increasingly decouple equipment procurement from OEM-controlled service contracts. The report also links independent servicing credibility to ISO 13485:2016 certification, which could become a procurement argument for third-party providers seeking to professionalize and scale.

**Strategic relevance:** Trend indicator / Risk  
Independent service providers are actively challenging OEM control over ultrasound maintenance economics. For imaging OEMs, this increases pressure to demonstrate measurable value in service contracts through uptime guarantees, cybersecurity, software continuity, quality assurance, and integrated fleet analytics rather than relying primarily on OEM exclusivity.
