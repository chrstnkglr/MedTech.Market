## FDA authorizes more devices so far in 2026, but it’s taking longer

**Author:** Elise Reuter  
**Source:** [MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/fda-authorizes-more-devices-so-far-in-2026-but-its-taking-longer/824233/)  
**Publication date:** July 1, 2026

MedTech Dive reports that FDA medical device authorizations are running ahead of the prior year, but approval and clearance timelines have lengthened. According to BTIG’s analysis cited in the article, original PMA approvals increased in the first half of 2026, while average PMA review time rose materially. The average 510(k) clearance timeline also increased, though less sharply, reaching roughly 156 days in the first half of the year. De novo classifications remained broadly in line with last year, but with longer approval timing.

For imaging and digital-health competitors, the key issue is execution predictability. AI-enabled imaging software, advanced visualization, workflow tools, and modality upgrades often rely on 510(k), de novo, or PMA pathways depending on risk classification and claims. Even moderate timeline slippage can affect product launch windows, customer commitments, evidence-generation planning, and revenue phasing. The effect is likely more material for focused AI and software challengers than for larger imaging OEMs with diversified installed-base revenue and broader regulatory teams.

**Strategic relevance:** **Trend indicator / Risk** — A more congested or slower FDA pathway may raise the value of regulatory scale, submission quality, and portfolio diversification, potentially reinforcing advantages for established MedTech and imaging players while increasing commercialization risk for smaller AI and imaging-software entrants.
